# Privacy Policy — BZ Music

**Last updated:** October 2, 2026  
**App:** BZ Music (`com.borislav007.muzika`)  
**Developer:** Borislav Zahariev (BZ)

## Overview

BZ Music is a local music player. It reads audio that Android already indexes in your device Media library and plays it on your device. It is not a cloud music service and does not require an account. It can also play WMA files.

## Data we access on your device

- **Music / audio files (MediaStore):** With your permission (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` on Android 13+, or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on older versions), BZ Music lists and plays tracks from the system media library. Playback uses content URIs; the app does not request “All files access.”
- **App preferences:** Shuffle/repeat and the last selected folder path are stored locally on the device.

## Internet use

- BZ Music may use the network **only** to look up album artwork when a track has no embedded cover image.
- No login, analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are included for that purpose.

## Data we do not collect

BZ Music does not collect, sell, or share:

- Personal identity or contact information
- Location
- Payment or financial data
- Account credentials
- Audio files uploaded to a server

Audio stays on your device unless you choose to share files yourself outside the app.

## Children

BZ Music is a general-audience music player. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
